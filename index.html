The Assignment & The Tools

Kingdom Laws for Purpose, Excellence and Business.

In The Assignment & The Tools, Isaiah Adegoke explores the relationship between calling, preparation, and excellence. Written for believers, professionals, and ministry leaders, the book considers how faith can guide purposeful decisions in work, business, and service.

Having the tools without knowing the assignment is as frustrating as having an assignment without knowing which tool to use. Discover how purpose unfolds in progressive phases and how disciplined preparation empowers meaningful impact.
