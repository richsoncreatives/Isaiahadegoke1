Overall assessment
The website provides a strong foundation for presenting your professional expertise, ministry and authorship. Its
visual identity is coherent, its navigation is straightforward, and its content gives visitors substantial background on
your work. Before final approval, I recommend correcting several download and content destinations, reconciling
credentials and research status, and tightening the copy around the audiences you most want to reach.
The central communications opportunity is to make the relationship between your roles easier to understand and
give each audience a clear next step. Selected evidence, precise descriptions and shorter passages will strengthen
credibility more effectively than repeated assertions of excellence.
Purpose and review basis
This review covers writing, messaging, page structure, credibility, calls to action and visible desktop usability. It
assumes the website aims to build professional credibility, attract speaking and consulting enquiries, sell books and
grow the ministry audience. Please confirm which objective takes priority before substantial rewriting.
How to use this report
Essential items should be resolved before final approval. Recommended items would materially improve clarity or
visitor experience. Optional items are refinements. Observed inconsistencies and click outcomes are distinguished
from factual claims requiring your confirmation and technical services requiring developer verification.
Immediate priorities
● Correct the resource download and speaker kit destinations.
● Confirm publication status, exact credential names and the evidence behind numerical achievements.
● Verify checkout, fulfilment and enquiry delivery before approving the associated promises.
● Simplify the opening messages and provide clear routes for readers, event organisers and prospective consulting
clients.

Home and About
Home
Essential | Research status. The homepage refers to peer-reviewed research on AI-enhanced corporate
sustainability, while Experience lists the relevant manuscript as under peer review. Confirm whether the statements
refer to the same work and align the wording with its current status.
Recommended | Opening message and audience routes. The introduction lists several roles but gives limited
explanation of how visitors can engage you. Keep the brand statement, then explain your work plainly and provide
separate routes for consulting, speaking and books. Replace the combined “Book Consultation / Speaking” action
with clearer individual actions.
Suggested wording: Isaiah Adegoke is a quality assurance engineer, sustainability scholar, pastor and author. His
work connects process improvement, purposeful leadership and faith, bringing practical insights to organisations,
professional communities and congregations.
Recommended | Book section and repetition. “Latest books” introduces one featured book, while “Featured
assets” sounds like an internal marketing label. Use “Featured book”, shorten the homepage description and direct
readers to the Library page for details.
Recommended | Episode destinations. Homepage episode links lead to the general Speaking page, which initially
displays Pneuma Studios content. Ask the developer to open the corresponding episode directly.
Optional | Unclear terminology. Replace “sanitation-grade discipline” with a precise description such as “sanitation
controls and quality assurance in continuous production operations”, if accurate.
About
Essential | Degree titles and dates. The undergraduate qualification appears as “Theology & Management” here
and “Theology with Management Science” on Experience. The study period ends in 2014, while an award is listed
under 2015. Check the official title and distinguish study completion, graduation and award dates as needed. Kindly
ensure it is consistent with your CV entries.
Essential | Award selection criteria. Confirm the precise basis for the statement that Hamand Society membership
is restricted to the university’s top five graduate students. Link to the official criteria where available and avoid
stronger wording than those criteria support.
Recommended | Biography length. Break the biography into short sections covering professional work, ministry,
and family and community service. Retain the personal warmth while reducing repeated descriptions of purpose and
excellence.
Recommended | Names and academic framing. Confirm Pearl’s preferred form of address and use “Dr Pearl
Adegoke” or “Pearl Adegoke, PhD” consistently. “Three campuses, four degrees” is not inherently inconsistent; “Four
degrees across three continents” is an optional clarity improvement. Reduce repeated emphasis on the two separate
master’s programmes.

Experience and professional credibility
The Experience page contains useful detail, but readers need clearer distinctions between verified achievements,
research outputs and qualifications. These recommendations concern precision and presentation; they do not
establish that any underlying claim is inaccurate.
Essential | Numerical achievements. Claims include 100% compliance, zero contamination, 20% profitability growth
and a 35% reduction in reporting turnaround time. Confirm the underlying records and specify the measure, period
and scope. Clarify whether a result relates to an individual shift, production line, project or whole operation.
Confirm that employer-related information is approved for public use where necessary.
Essential | Overlapping roles. Healthy Haven employment overlaps with overseas study and university employment.
I think it will be helpful to explain whether the role was full-time, part-time, remote, advisory or undertaken through
another arrangement. A brief qualifier can prevent an otherwise valid timeline from appearing unclear.
Essential | Research categories. Separate published journal articles, manuscripts under review, independent
research and conference presentations. A manuscript under review should not be presented as an accepted or
published peer-reviewed article.
Recommended | Complete research references. Add author names, publication year, exact title, journal, volume
and pages or article number, and a DOI or publisher link where available. Use one citation format across the site and
reconcile the overall publication count with the categories included.
Recommended | Certification names. The homepage uses “Black Belt / Green Belt”, while Experience uses “Black
Belt”. “Certified Quality Assurance Engineer” also becomes “Quality Assurance Engineer”. Reproduce the exact
certificate titles and issuing organisations consistently.
Recommended | Awards headings. Replace “Honors, strictly separated by discipline” with “Awards and
recognition”. Technology and Sustainability can remain useful subheadings. Remove emphatic terms such as “elite”
or “strictly” unless needed to convey documented selection criteria.
Recommended | Experience bullets. Replace inflated descriptions such as “an unyielding culture” and “high-velocity
cross-functional synchronization” with specific actions and outcomes. Use present tense for continuing
responsibilities and past tense for completed work.
Suggested wording: Trained production teams in standard operating procedures, sanitation controls and continuous
improvement practices.
Presenting measured results
Use a consistent structure for achievements: action, defined outcome and measurement period. For example,
“Reduced [defined measure] by [verified percentage] over [period] through [specific intervention].” The bracketed
fields are prompts for verification and should not appear in the published copy.
Records to confirm
Check degree certificates, award notifications, professional certifications, publication records and the source of each
numerical claim. Supporting links can be selective; the website does not need to reproduce every record.

Insights and Contact
Insights
Essential | Absolute contamination claim. The Healthy Haven case study says contamination risks were eliminated
“entirely”. Replace this with the precise, evidenced outcome. A record of no observed contamination over a defined
period does not establish that all risk was eliminated.
Essential | Financial terminology. Related results are described as profitability, gross margins and gross profitability
across the site. Confirm the actual financial measure and use it consistently, including whether the change is a
percentage increase or a change in percentage points.
Recommended | Reading estimates. The six-minute essay and eight-minute case study estimates appear high
relative to the short texts opened. Recalculate estimates from the complete published content.
Recommended | Grammar and references. Change “You cannot improve a chaos” to “You cannot improve a process
you have not defined.” Add chapter, verse and translation details for the Philippians quotation and verify its wording.
Use consistent title capitalisation, including words such as “Is” and “Be” if applying title case.
Recommended | Article access and evidence. The inspected articles open in overlays while the page URL remains
unchanged. Give each article a permanent URL so readers can share and cite it. Link research-based pieces to their
sources and distinguish research findings from personal interpretation.
Contact
Essential | Response expectations. The page promises a response within “48 business hours”, while speaking
invitations are reviewed weekly. Confirm the intended commitments and state them separately. If two business days
is intended, kindly say so directly.
Suggested wording: General enquiries are usually answered within two business days. Speaking invitations are
reviewed weekly; please allow up to five business days for a response.
Adopt these timeframes only if they reflect the actual service you can provide.
Recommended | Labels and inclusive wording. Use “Phone” instead of “Phone & Cell”, “Based in” instead of
“Location Base”, and “audience” or “attendees” instead of “congregation or delegation” where the instruction
applies to all event types.
Recommended | Enquiry routing. Explain when to use the general contact form and when to use the dedicated
speaking form. Ask the developer to verify that both deliver messages to the appropriate recipient.
Desktop presentation
The inspected desktop views use a coherent navy, burgundy and cream palette, prominent headings and
straightforward navigation. Professional imagery supports the personal brand. A skip-to-content link and descriptive
text for several images are positive features. Contact-form labels and placeholder text appeared faint in the captured
view; contrast should be measured before treating this as a confirmed accessibility failure.

Implementation and review scope
Site wide editorial consistency
Choose one English convention and apply it consistently. The site mixes British forms such as “honours” and
“programmes” with American forms such as “honors” and “rigor”. American English is a reasonable option for a
US-based professional, subject to your preference. Preserve official qualification names and publication titles when
standardising the surrounding copy.
Reduce repeated uses of purpose, excellence, discipline, stewardship and impact where a concrete description
would be more informative. Selected publication links, accurate credentials and approved testimonials or event
examples, if available, would make credibility easier to assess.
A branded domain is an optional refinement for the final public site. The current developer-hosted address may be
appropriate during review.
Responsibilities and sequence
● Isaiah: confirm the site’s primary audience and objective, credentials, publication status, dates, numerical
claims, resource availability and response commitments.
● Editorial consultant: reconcile terminology, tighten page copy, revise calls to action and apply a consistent style
after factual confirmation.
● Developer: correct resource and episode destinations; verify checkout, digital delivery, shipping information,
contact forms and newsletter subscription; provide permanent article URLs where agreed.
● Developer: test mobile layouts, menu behaviour, keyboard access, modal focus and closing controls, form labels
and colour contrast. These require a dedicated usability and accessibility check.
● Isaiah and developer: confirm appropriate privacy, shipping and refund information for the actual services
offered. No links to these were apparent in the inspected navigation or footers.
● Final review: check revised wording and destinations together before approving the site.
Scope and limitations
The review covered Home, About, Experience, Speaking and Podcast, Library, Insights and Contact. It included the
homepage audience tabs, both awards panels, the speaking sections and enquiry-form presentation, two article
readers and a checkout overlay. Observations reflect the version accessed during the review on 8 November 2026;
later changes may resolve or alter the findings.
No contact or subscription forms were submitted. No payment details were entered and no purchase was
completed. Credentials, employment history, awards and research claims were not independently authenticated
against external records. The downloadable CV and external retailer destinations were not assessed. Audio playback,
every article, every resource and every external link were not tested.
This is an editorial and communications assessment with limited desktop usability observations. It is not a security,
legal compliance, search performance or comprehensive accessibility audit. Mobile-device behaviour was not
tested. Proposed wording is illustrative and should be approved for factual accuracy before publication.
