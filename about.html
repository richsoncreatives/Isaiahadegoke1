About · The Journey

From Accra to Nanjing to the American heartland: a journey of disciplined purpose

Two master's degrees, an MBA and a first-class honours degree across three continents, a marriage that anchors it all, and one conviction: excellence is a form of faithfulness.

Isaiah & Dr. Mrs. Pearl Adegoke (PhD) at graduation — "This far by grace."

Isaiah Adegoke

Isaiah is a Quality Assurance Engineer, process improvement leader and Lean Six Sigma professional whose career runs through beverage manufacturing facilities at Coca-Cola Southwest Beverages in Texas, commercial juice production in Accra and university student communities in Illinois. In each setting the pattern is the same: find the hidden inefficiency, build the standard, and train people to hold it.

He is also an ordained pastor and founder of Pneuma Studios. Raised in Ghana, shaped by church leadership, campus fellowships and youth ministry from his teens, he learned early that people follow those whose lives are coherent. That conviction now drives his writing, his podcast The Believer's Corner, Morning Glory devotional broadcasts, and his speaking engagements with professionals, scholars and congregations worldwide.

Married life is the quiet engine behind all of it. Isaiah is married to Dr. Mrs. Pearl Adegoke (PhD), and together they sum up the journey with the words embroidered on the stole she wore at graduation: "This far by grace." Dr. Pearl is his closest counsel, his first reader and an inspiring partner in the calling they carry as a family — a warmth and constancy that shape everything he writes, teaches and builds.

That calling is lived out in service, and much of it they give together. Isaiah and Dr. Pearl devote their time to mentorship, youth development and campus ministry, believing that strong communities are built person by person. Isaiah has facilitated training for resident assistants and student leaders, conducted marriage and relationship workshops, and served congregations across three continents.

Academic Credentials

Three campuses, four degrees, three continents of learning

Each degree was not a trophy to collect, but an intellectual discipline to master.

2023 – 2025

MSc, Technology

Master's programme 1 of 2 Hamand Society of Graduate Scholars · Reserved for Top 5 Graduate Students National Robotics Challenge (NRC) Bronze Medalist

Eastern Illinois University, Charleston, USA

The first of two separate master's programmes completed at Eastern Illinois University. Grounded in technology: quality systems, process improvement, Lean Six Sigma operations, and the discipline of standards that hold up on an industrial production floor. His graduate scholarship was crowned by the King-Mertz Graduate Research Excellence Award, winning the Bronze Medal at the National Robotics Challenge (NRC) for autonomous sumo robot engineering, and induction into the prestigious Hamand Society of Graduate Scholars—an elite academic honor strictly reserved for only the top 5 graduate students across the entire University.

2023 – 2025

MSc, Sustainability

Master's programme 2 of 2 Distinguished Graduate Student Award · 2025

Eastern Illinois University, Charleston, USA

A distinct master's programme, pursued concurrently alongside the MSc in Technology and conferred the same year. Here Isaiah studied sustainability as an operating discipline: stewardship, environmental leadership, carbon metrics and the business case for organizations that endure. His research examined AI-enabled corporate sustainability (using Belvedere Trading as a primary case study), recognized with the Distinguished Graduate Student Award in Sustainability in 2025.

2020 – 2022

MBA, Business Management

International Scholar

Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology, China

Two rigorous years in Jiangsu province as an international scholar on full government scholarship. Here Isaiah honed his strategic thinking, published papers on sustainable supply chains and SME innovation, and served as Vice President of Academic Affairs for international scholars, bridging cross-cultural teams with structured accountability.

2010 – 2014

BA (Hons), Theology & Management, First Class

Best Graduating Student Award

Regent University College of Science and Technology, Accra, Ghana

The foundation of the tri-fold calling: a first-class honours degree uniting theology with management sciences, recognized with the Best Graduating Student Award in the department. It is where faith, organizational governance, and marketplace stewardship first converged into a lifelong philosophy.

Core Operating Philosophy

Where faith, improvement and discipline intersect

Faith gives the why

Work is a stewardship. Purpose is not found by accident, it is received, clarified and then pursued with discipline.

Continuous improvement is humility in action

Lean thinking says every process can be better. The same is true of character. Kaizen and sanctification rhyme.

Structure sustains vision

Standards, audits and systems are not the enemy of calling. They are the scaffolding that lets calling scale and endure.
