The Assignment & The Tools

Kingdom Laws for Purpose, Excellence and Business.

You have the tools. But do you know the assignment?

Many people will die with their gifts still inside them. Not because they lacked talent, but because they never understood why they were given what they have. Having the tools without knowing the assignment is as frustrating as having an assignment without knowing which tool to use.

In The Assignment & The Tools, Isaiah Adegoke reveals the divine order that governs a fulfilled life. You will learn why purpose is unveiled in phases, why your gift is self-supporting, and why excellence is not an optional skill but a spiritual responsibility that must be stewarded.

This book is for every believer, marketplace professional, and ministry leader who knows they were created for more but wants a clear, actionable roadmap to fulfill their calling with disciplined execution.
